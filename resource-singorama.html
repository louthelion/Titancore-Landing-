Singorama — an honest look

TitanCore Resources ShelfPrices checked November 2026

Affiliate disclosure: TitanCore Holdings may earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. We only list products we've reviewed against our standards, and we say what we don't like, too.

What it is. Singorama 2.0 is a downloadable singing course created by vocal coach Melanie Alexander. It is an audio-led program: 28 step-by-step vocal lessons supported by ebooks and worksheets, bundled with practice software — an advancement app with more than 120 warm-up and technique tracks, a mini recording studio for tracking your own progress, an ear-training program (Perfect Your Pitch Pro), and a guide to reading music.

Who it fits. Beginners and self-taught singers who want a structured practice routine for tone, pitch, rhythm, and range, and who are comfortable practicing with a phone and headphones. Who should skip it: anyone who wants a teacher listening to their voice — no recorded course can hear you — and singers who learn mainly by watching technique demonstrated on video, because Singorama's teaching is mostly audio.

How it works. The 28 lessons move from the foundations — posture, breathing, voice care — through technique such as staying in tune, bridging registers, and extending range, into essential music theory and singing styles, and finish with how to break down and learn new songs. Four bonus audio sets target warm-ups, vocal strengthening, range extension, and interval training. After checkout you download the lessons and software and work through them at your own pace, using the recording studio and pitch software to check yourself between lessons.

What we liked. The scope for the price. A full curriculum plus practice software for one one-time payment costs less than a single month of weekly private lessons usually does. The structure is genuinely step-by-step rather than a pile of tips, and the self-check tools — record your own voice, train your ear — address the biggest weakness of learning alone.

What's weak. Three things, stated plainly. First, the teaching is audio-first; if you need to see technique, modern video courses do that better. Second, the maker's sales page is long, dated, and buries its own price — and after checkout you may be offered an optional "Platinum Inner Circle" membership (a free first month, then a monthly charge). It is optional, but read each checkout screen before you click. Third, no software replaces a coach's ears: the program trains your own listening; it cannot correct you personally.

The claims line. The maker advertises professional-level results and range gains of "an octave or more." We don't repeat outcome promises — voices change with consistent practice, at individual speeds. What we can verify is the curriculum and the tools, above.

Price and guarantee. $67 as a one-time payment for Singorama 2.0 with all bonuses, as shown on the maker's order page when we checked in November 2026. The page also shows higher struck-through "values" — those are the maker's own anchors, not the price. The optional Inner Circle trial is separate, and recurring if you accept it and don't cancel. Purchases carry a 60-day money-back guarantee.

Bottom line. A broad, structured vocal course with real practice tools at a one-time price — best for self-motivated beginners who are happy learning by ear. If you decide to buy, the button below supports the free resources on this site at no extra cost to you.
