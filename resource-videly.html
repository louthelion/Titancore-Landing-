Videly — an honest look

TitanCore Resources ShelfPrices checked November 2026

Affiliate disclosure: TitanCore Holdings may earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. We only list tools we've reviewed against our standards, and we say what we don't like, too.

What it is. Videly is a cloud-based video SEO research tool from Blaster Suite, a software team known for marketer tools. It doesn't make videos. It helps you decide what a video should target and how it should be packaged: find keywords worth chasing, study what's already ranking, and generate titles, descriptions, and tags to match.

Who it fits. Local businesses and creators who publish on YouTube regularly — a restaurant showing the kitchen, a contractor showing jobs, a shop showing products — and who currently guess at titles and tags. Who should skip it: anyone who posts rarely, because the research time saved won't repay the price; anyone expecting software to rank a weak video; and anyone working outside YouTube and Google video, which is the ground this tool covers.

How it works. You enter a topic, and Videly returns related keywords with search-volume and competitor-view data, drawing on sources that include YouTube, Google, and Amazon, with country targeting for local research. Pick a keyword, and its generator drafts a title, description, and tag set for the video. That keyword finder and generator are the base tier. Two features often shown in demonstrations — niche analysis (how crowded a topic is) and rank tracking (watching your videos' positions on Google and YouTube) — sit in paid upgrades, Pro and Agency, offered after checkout, so the full toolset costs more than the headline price. There is no free trial.

What we liked. The workflow is genuinely useful for a small business: one place to check demand before filming, instead of shooting a video nobody searches for. The base tier is a one-time payment rather than another monthly subscription, and the research covers the platforms a local business actually cares about.

What's weak — read this before buying. Two honest flags. First, momentum: independent checks in late 2026 found the maker's own websites displaying "sold out / not accepting new users" notices even while the ClickBank checkout remained live, and recent sales activity is low. Long-term support and updates are therefore a genuine open question, and the refund window below is your protection. Second, scope: the base tier is research and packaging only. Ranking still depends on the video itself — its content, its competition, and whether people watch it.

The claims line. The maker's own advertising promises first-page rankings. No tool can promise that, and we don't. Videly is a research aid: it can point your effort at terms people actually search. What a video earns from there is earned by the video.

Price and guarantee. $47 as a one-time payment for the base tier, as shown at checkout when we checked in November 2026; upgrades are optional extras offered afterward. Purchases made through ClickBank carry ClickBank's standard 60-day refund window.

Bottom line. A practical research tool at a fair one-time price, with a support question mark we've stated plainly. If you publish often and decide to buy, the button below supports the free resources on this site at no extra cost to you.
